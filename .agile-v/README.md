# 🏃‍♂️ QuickBite Agile Workspace

Welcome to the Agile/Scrum workspace for **QuickBite - Multi-Vendor Food Delivery SaaS**. 
This directory (`.agile-v`) houses all our sprint planning, backlogs, and scrum artifacts.

> [!NOTE]
> We follow a 2-week Sprint cycle to iteratively build, test, and ship features.

## 📂 Directory Structure

- 📜 [Product Backlog](./PRODUCT_BACKLOG.md) - The master list of all Epics and User Stories.
- 🎯 [Sprint 01](./SPRINT_01.md) - Foundation & Core Architecture
- 🎯 [Sprint 02](./SPRINT_02.md) - Restaurant & Menu Management
- 🤝 [Scrum Events & Templates](./SCRUM_EVENTS.md) - Daily standup templates and meeting notes.

## 👥 Scrum Team Roles

| Role | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| 👑 **Product Owner** | Manages the Product Backlog, ensures business value. |
| 🛡️ **Scrum Master** | Facilitates ceremonies, removes blockers. |
| 💻 **Development Team** | Builds the platform (Frontend, Backend, Mobile). |

## 🔄 Scrum Ceremonies

1. **Sprint Planning:** Define the Sprint Goal and select Backlog items.
2. **Daily Scrum:** 15-minute daily sync (What I did, What I will do, Blockers).
3. **Sprint Review:** Demo the increment to stakeholders.
4. **Sprint Retrospective:** What went well, what to improve.
